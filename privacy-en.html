Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-12

Introduction

Bonyani respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect when you use the app, how we use and protect it, and your rights.

Data we collect

• Account: name, email, phone number and account type (individual or company). • Companies: company name, commercial license number, specialties, service areas, products and prices, and photos you upload. • Your project: area, block and plot, budget, Credit Bank loan, stages, expenses and receipt photos. • Quote requests, offers, contracts, payments and reviews. • Market listings you post: title, description, price, photos, contact number and governorate. • Contract consultation requests: the type, your description, the contract file you upload or scan with the camera, your preferred time, your name and phone number for contact, and the office's reply and notes. • Support messages you send. We use the camera only when you photograph a receipt or scan a contract. Calculator estimates you save stay on your device. We don't use your device's location (GPS); the area, block and plot you enter are stored with your project. Ads in the app are posted by Bonyani and companies on Bonyani; we use no third-party ad networks or tracking tools, and ads aren't targeted using your data.

How we use it

• To run your account and sync your data across your devices. • To deliver your request to matching companies in your area, and their offers to you. • To deliver a consultation request to approved law offices, then to the office that takes it. • To send the notifications you turned on (new offers, accepted offers, consultation updates, maintenance reminders). • To answer support tickets and review companies before approval. We never sell your data or share it for marketing.

What others see

• Matching companies see your request details (trade, governorate, description, area and budget if given), not your email or phone. When you accept a company's offer, that company alone sees your name and phone number to arrange the work. • Approved law offices see an open consultation request (type, description, governorate, preferred time) without your name or phone, and can't open the contract file. Once an office takes your request it sees your name, phone number and contract file so it can contact you and review it; no other office does. • Approved company profiles, products, prices, deals and photos are public, including the phone and WhatsApp number on the company profile (the contact person's number entered at company sign-up, unless changed in the Company Dashboard). • Market listings are public, including their photos and the contact number you put in them, until you delete them or mark them sold. • Reviews are public and show your first name only. • Your project, expenses and contracts are private to you.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with the cloud provider Supabase and sent over encrypted connections. Access rules let each user see only their own data and what they're allowed to see. Authorised Bonyani staff access account data only to provide support, review companies and moderate content. Account emails (confirmation, password reset) are sent through Google's Gmail service. We keep data while your account exists; support messages sent without an account are kept with the contact you gave so we can reply, and you can ask us to delete them.

Your rights

• Edit your details in Menu (⋯) → My Account → Edit Profile. • Permanently delete your account and data in Menu (⋯) → My Account → Delete account, including your project, requests, contracts, consultations and support messages. • Control notifications in Menu (⋯) → Notifications. • Contact us with any question about your data.

Children

The app is for adults. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy and will let you know in the app about any significant change.

Contact

Through Menu (⋯) → Contact & Help, or support WhatsApp.
