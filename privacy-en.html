Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-07

Introduction

Bonyani respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect when you use the app, how we use and protect it, and your rights.

Data we collect

• Account: name, email, phone number and account type (individual or company). • Companies: company name, commercial license number, specialties, service areas, products and prices, and photos you upload. • Your project: area, block and plot, budget, Credit Bank loan, stages, expenses and receipt photos. • Quote requests, offers, contracts, payments and reviews. • Support messages you send. We don't collect your location, and we use no ads or tracking tools.

How we use it

• To run your account and sync your data across your devices. • To deliver your request to matching companies in your area, and their offers to you. • To send the notifications you turned on (new offers, accepted offers, maintenance reminders). • To answer support tickets and review companies before approval. We never sell your data or share it for marketing.

What others see

• Matching companies see your request details (trade, governorate, description, area and budget if given), not your email or phone. • Approved company profiles, products and photos are public. • Reviews are public and show your first name only. • Your project, expenses and contracts are private to you.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with the cloud provider Supabase and sent over encrypted connections. Access rules let each user see only their own data and what they're allowed to see. We keep data while your account exists.

Your rights

• Edit your details in Account → Edit Profile. • Permanently delete your account and data in Account → Delete account. • Control notifications in Account → Notifications. • Contact us with any question about your data.

Children

The app is for adults. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy and will let you know in the app about any significant change.

Contact

Through Account → Help & Support, or support WhatsApp.
